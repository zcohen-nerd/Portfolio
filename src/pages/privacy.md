# Privacy

`portfolio.zcohen-nerd.com` is a static site. It loads **no analytics, no
cookies, and no third-party scripts**, and it writes nothing to `localStorage` or
`sessionStorage`. All CSS, JavaScript, images, and fonts are served from this
domain.

Links out to other sites — GitHub, LinkedIn, Substack, the connector guide, DOI
records, vendor datasheets — take you to those sites under **their** privacy
policies, not this one.

The shared footer and identity across the zcohen-nerd ecosystem are covered by the
hub privacy note: **[zcohen-nerd.com/privacy](https://zcohen-nerd.com/privacy/)**
(that site uses cookieless [Plausible](https://plausible.io/) analytics).
